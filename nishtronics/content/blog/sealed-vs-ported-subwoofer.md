Two identical subwoofers can sound completely different in different boxes. Before you choose a subwoofer, it helps to know which kind of enclosure you want to put it in.

## Sealed enclosures

A sealed box is airtight. The air inside acts like a spring that controls the cone.

- Bass is tight, controlled and accurate.
- It copes well with a wide range of music.
- It is usually smaller, which matters when you also want a usable boot.
- It is more forgiving if the box volume is slightly off.
- It needs more power to reach the same loudness as a ported box.

## Ported enclosures

A ported box has a vent, called a port, tuned to a particular frequency. Around that frequency the port adds output, so the subwoofer gets louder for the same power.

- It is louder and more efficient near the tuning frequency.
- Bass is deeper and fuller, which some people hear as boomy.
- It is bigger, and the port needs to be built to the right dimensions.
- If it is tuned badly, bass can sound sloppy.
- Below the tuning frequency the cone is less controlled, so very low notes need care.

## Which one for your music

As a starting point:

- **Rock, jazz, acoustic, vocals and most pop:** sealed usually sounds more natural.
- **Hip-hop, dancehall, electronic and anything with long, deep bass notes:** ported often suits it better.

Your taste is the real deciding factor. If you listen to a bit of everything and want one safe choice, sealed is the easier one to live with.

## Get the volume right

Every subwoofer has a recommended enclosure volume in its specification sheet, for both sealed and ported use. Follow it. Guessing is how people end up with a subwoofer that sounds worse than the one it replaced.

Remember that the figure is **net** volume, the air space inside after you subtract the subwoofer itself, the port and any bracing.

## Building it to last

Roads are rough, and a box that flexes loses bass and starts to buzz. Good enclosures use thick MDF, glued and screwed joints, internal bracing and sealed seams. If the space in your car is awkward, a custom fibreglass enclosure can follow the shape of the boot or spare-wheel well and still hold the right volume.
