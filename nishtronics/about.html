Who We Are

Nairobi's
Audio Experts

We're Nairobi's specialist car audio and home theatre shop. From a simple radio swap to a full competition SPL build — and everything in between.

We stock everyday brands like Kenwood, Pioneer, and Nakamichi, plus premium names like Sundown Audio, Kicker, Rockford Fosgate, and Taramps for builds that go all out.

Our team also handles car tracker installation, window tinting, car wraps, custom subwoofer box fabrication, and plug-and-play systems. If it's audio or car accessories, we do it.
